FAQ

Questions

Do I need WSL or admin rights?

No. SSH Deck ships its own Cygwin with OpenSSH and installs per user. If you have WSL, it works too.

Where are my SSH keys?

In your own ~/.ssh and in the Windows ssh-agent. SSH Deck never uploads them anywhere.

Does the AI see my servers?

Only when you ask it something, and only the latest terminal lines, with secrets masked. With a local model nothing leaves your PC.

Which AI providers work?

Ollama and LM Studio (local), Anthropic Claude and any OpenAI-compatible API with your own key.
